Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026 · CoreTools by JDG Initiates

  • CoreTools runs on your device. There are no CoreTools servers, no accounts with us, no analytics and no telemetry.
  • We, the developers, never receive your files, text, codes or secrets.
  • The only feature that uses the internet is the optional Google Drive backup in the Authenticator. It is off until you connect it, and it uploads only data encrypted on your device with your own password.

1. Who we are

CoreTools is a free desktop and in-browser toolkit published by JDG Initiates (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the CoreTools desktop application, the in-browser version at /app, and this website.

2. The tools process your data locally

Diff-Checker, YML-Validator, Grammar-Polisher and JSON-Studio process everything on your device. They make no network requests: nothing you load, paste, type or generate is sent to us or to anyone else. Files are read only when you open them and written only when you choose where to save them.

3. Data stored on your device

  • Authenticator keeps your two-factor accounts in local storage on your device so they survive restarts. Once you set an encryption password, every secret is encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a key derived from that password. The password itself is never stored.
  • JSON-Studio keeps an autosave of the document you are editing so it can be recovered after a crash; you can discard it at any time.
  • The other tools store nothing. Wipe all data in Authenticator settings deletes everything the Authenticator stored on the device.

4. Optional Google Drive backup (Authenticator and Credential Manager, desktop app)

If you choose Connect Google Account, CoreTools backs up your Authenticator accounts and Credential Manager logins — each as its own set of files — to your own Google Drive so you can restore them after reinstalling or on a new device. You sign in with Google in your own web browser; CoreTools never sees your Google password.

What CoreTools accesses in your Google account

  • Your email address (openid, email), only to show which account is connected.
  • A private, hidden app folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). CoreTools can create, read and delete only its own backup files there. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

What is stored there, and who can read it

  • Each backup is encrypted on your device, before upload, with AES-256-GCM under a key derived from your Authenticator encryption password. This covers your secrets and also account names and settings.
  • Neither we nor Google can read the contents. We operate no server and never receive your backups or Google data. Google can see only that the files exist, plus their dates, sizes and the computer name used to label them.
  • Restoring requires your encryption password and, where your device supports it, a Windows Hello check.
  • Versions are kept so you can roll back: the newest 10, then one per hour for a day, one per day for a month and one per month for a year. Older ones are deleted automatically.

Where your Google sign-in is kept

The access granted by Google is stored in your operating system's secure credential store (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain or the Linux Secret Service) on your device. It is used only to read and write the backup files described above.

That store belongs to your user account rather than to the app, so the sign-in stays if you simply uninstall, and a reinstall shows the account as connected. On Windows, ticking Delete the application data when uninstalling removes it along with your local data.

How to stop and delete

  • Disconnect account removes the sign-in from your device and revokes CoreTools' access at Google.
  • Delete cloud backups permanently removes every backup file from your Drive.
  • You can also revoke access at any time under your Google Account → Security → Third-party connections.

Google API Services User Data Policy

CoreTools' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the backup and restore feature you turned on. It is never sold, never used for advertising, never transferred to others, and never read by people.

5. This website, downloads and update notifications

This website uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. It is hosted on Vercel, and installers are downloaded from GitHub; both may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP address and time of request) under their own privacy policies. We do not receive or combine those logs.

The desktop app can tell you when a new version is out. This is off until you turn it on. When on, the app reads one public file from GitHub when it opens and about once an hour while it stays open. The request carries only the app's version in its user agent — nothing about you, your device or your data — and GitHub may log it like any download. A new version is downloaded and installed only when you click Update now, and only if its signature proves it came from the CoreTools release process.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal data, because we do not collect any. The only third party involved is Google, and only when you connect Google Drive backup yourself.

7. Children

CoreTools is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We knowingly collect no data from anyone.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Material changes affecting Google Drive backup will also be noted in the release notes.
